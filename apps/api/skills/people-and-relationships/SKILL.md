# People and relationships

- Named people are entities typed as person, even when they are only mentioned.
- A role relation (manager, sister) can be stated in either direction. Pick the one the sentence states and keep it consistent.
- Third parties mentioned in passing still get entities. When the user reports what someone else said ("Dev says X is hiring"), extract it as a relation from that person, since it is reported, not confirmed.
